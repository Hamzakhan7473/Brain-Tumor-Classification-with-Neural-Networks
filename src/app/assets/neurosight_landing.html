Clinical pathway

Trust is earned
in phases.

No AI tool should jump straight to clinical decisions. NeuroSight defines a transparent, evidence-gated progression that regulators and hospital partners can follow.

01
Research mode
Retrospective datasets. No live patient data. Validation report pack generated. Evidence baseline established.
Active now
02
Shadow mode
AI runs in parallel with clinical workflow. Results logged but not shown to clinicians. Retrospective comparison builds evidence pack.
Active now
03
Assistive mode
AI draft shown to radiologist as a second opinion. Clinician confirms or overrides. Feedback loop active. No autonomous decision.
Phase 2
04
PACS integrated
Full DICOM routing, RIS integration, and regulatory clearance. Only after QMS, validation data, and regulatory strategy are complete.
